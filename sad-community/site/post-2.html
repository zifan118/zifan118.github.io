My morning routine for difficult days

My routine By Leo Read: 4 min Nov 23, 2025
My routine Coping strategies
On days when I wake up already anxious, I keep the morning very simple. I open the window and count ten breaths. I write one sentence about what I am worried about, so it lives on paper rather than only in my head. And I pick one tiny goal for the day, like asking one question in a seminar. If I manage it, good. If not, I try again tomorrow.
