Join Harborly
to help in a gentle way

Harborly is a student-led community design project for people living with social anxiety. We welcome activity volunteers and mental health professionals who want to support a calmer, low-pressure space — with empathy, safety, and clear boundaries.

Requirements (simple but important)

We keep the bar realistic — but we also take care of community safety. If you join, you should be comfortable with:

  • Respecting anonymity and privacy.
  • Being non-judgemental and patient with slow pacing.
  • Following moderation rules and escalation guidelines.
  • Understanding this is not emergency / clinical care.

Who is a good fit?

If you like building safe spaces, listening, and creating gentle routines, you’ll probably enjoy helping Harborly.

  • Volunteers: community hosts, event helpers, moderators.
  • Professionals: counsellors/therapists who can share psychoeducation.
  • Students: welcome — especially with empathy + responsibility.
