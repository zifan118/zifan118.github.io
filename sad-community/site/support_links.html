Support & Crisis Resources

If you’re feeling overwhelmed, panicked, or stuck in a spiral, you deserve support right now. This page lists immediate options and weekly support sessions — choose what feels safest.

⚠️ If you are in immediate danger

  • UK emergency services: 999
  • NHS 111 (mental health option): 111
  • Samaritans (24/7): 116 123
