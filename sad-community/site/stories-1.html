The first time I ordered coffee without rehearsing

I used to practise my order three times in my head. Last week I just said it.

Author: Anonymous member • Location: Leeds (student project) • Read: ~4 min • Topic: Social anxiety

Where it started

For years, ordering a coffee meant rehearsing the sentence over and over while the queue moved forward. Last Tuesday I noticed I had reached the counter without practising at all. I said 'a flat white, please', the barista smiled, and that was it. Nothing dramatic happened, and that was exactly the point. I am writing it down so I remember that small things can change quietly.
