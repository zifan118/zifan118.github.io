Authority Without Authorship

Where does the human judgment go?

AI tools are sold as replacing film crews, editors and designers. So where does the human judgment that makes them work go?

How does AI-product marketing represent the labour of the people who operate the tools?

Using AI content tools involves repeated testing and selection. I know this first-hand from working as an AI content operator. Yet the marketing foregrounds generation time and leaves that testing uncounted.

A third way for labour to disappear

Existing accounts explain invisible labour in two ways. This study examines a third.
