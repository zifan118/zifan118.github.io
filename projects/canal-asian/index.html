Survey

Snowball-sampled online survey, then five semi-structured interviews. Each ring is a share of all 36 respondents.

Three rounds of user testing below

User testing · Round 1 of 3

Tester

I didn't know where to start.

Our change

Removed the country sidebar. Black and Canal+ red only.

Browsing by country lost viewers with no regional preference. We cut it, and cut the palette to black and red.

User testing · Round 2 of 3

Testers, in summary

The brand reads clearly now, but the culture has gone, and the programmes lead nowhere.

Our change

Added Explore and Discussion, show pop-ups and a watchlist.

The schedule grid lowered the commitment threshold, but cultural features had disappeared, so we brought them back as pages of their own.

User testing · Round 3 of 3

The final four-page site was validated.
