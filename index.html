Zifan
Zeng曾子凡

Final-year BA Digital Media student at the University of Leeds. I research platforms, algorithms and digital identity, with a focus on the labour that digital products keep out of view.

CV GitHub

I research what platforms make visible, and what they quietly leave out.

My work looks at how recommendation systems shape choice, how people perform identity across platforms, and how the human labour behind digital products is hidden from view.

I work across written research and making: content analysis and research design on one side, websites, prototypes and motion on the other. This portfolio gathers both.

Authority Without Authorship

How AI-product marketing makes the human judgment it depends on invisible. Drawing on a purposive sample of public marketing from AI vendors, the study proposes a third mechanism of invisible labour: invisibility demanded by the product's own claims.
